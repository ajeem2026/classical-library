      How this is reckoned

      Only seconds actually heard are entered; seeking, skipping and pausing add nothing. The mood is read from the music, not from you: minor and major come from each movement’s key in the catalogue; slow means a tempo marking of Andante or slower, fast Allegro or quicker (compound markings are spread evenly across the movement); quiet and loud are the softest and loudest thirds of the library, measured from the recordings in ten-second stretches; late night is 11 pm to 4 am. Movements without a key or tempo marking are left out of those shares.
